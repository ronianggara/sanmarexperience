GENERAL INFORMATION

  • This competition is designed for students who can read.
  • This competition is open to Elementary Students.
  • Participants that will be competing are 6th grade students.
  • Competition consists of 2 Rounds:
    • Preliminary Round
    • Final Round

PRELIMINARY ROUND

  1. The Preliminary Round will be conducted in a closed space.
  2. For each group, participants will be called one by one to enter the competition room.
  3. Each participant will be given a reading passage to be read clearly and loudly.
  4. Before commencing reading, participants will be given 15 seconds to familiarise themselves with the passage.
  5. Participants' reading performance will be timed.
  6. After reading, each participant will then receive two comprehension questions from the judge(s).
  7. Comprehension questions must be answered verbally in complete sentences.
  8. Participants' performance will be measured based on the following:
    • Accuracy of Pronunciation
    • Fluency and Pace of Reading
    • Intonation of Reading
    • Comprehension of Story
  9. If deemed unclear, judges reserve the right to ask participants to re-read words, re-answer comprehension questions or mark any words and/or answers wrong.
  10. Upon leaving the competition room, participants are prohibited from talking to the other participants.
  11. The best 6 participants will advance to the Final Round.
  12. Should two participants or more have the same performance results, the time it takes to read the passage will be taken into account.
  13. Tie-breaker rounds might be conducted when necessary.
  14. The judge's decision is final and cannot be contested.

FINAL ROUND

  1. The Final Round will be conducted in a closed space.
  2. Participants will be called on stage one by one.
  3. On the stage, each participant will be given a reading passage to be read clearly and loudly.
  4. Before commencing reading, participants will be given 15 seconds to familiarise themselves with the passage.
  5. Each participant will get a different reading passage: no passages will be used twice.
  6. After reading, each participant will then receive two comprehension questions from the judge(s).
  7. Comprehension questions must be answered verbally in complete sentences.
  8. Participants' performance will be measured based on the same criteria as the above.
  9. If deemed unclear, judges reserve the right to ask participants to re-read words, re-answer comprehension questions or mark any words and/or answers wrong.
  10. Upon leaving the stage, participants are prohibited from talking to the other participants.
  11. Tie-breaker rounds might be conducted when necessary.
  12. Tie-Breaker rounds will be conducted in a closed space.
  13. The judge's decision is final and cannot be contested.
